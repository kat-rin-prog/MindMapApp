Data-Driven Learning

In today’s fast-paced world, students face increasing challenges in maintaining concentration, processing information, and learning effectively. MindMap seeks to address these challenges by promoting personalized and evidence-based learning. The prototype integrates wearable brain-monitoring technology with memory retention assessments to investigate the impact of various study strategies on cognitive activity and learning outcomes.

How It Works

MindMap is a wearable neurotechnology device that helps students identify effective learning strategies by monitoring brain activity and testing memory retention.

fNIRS Technology

Employs functional near-infrared spectroscopy to monitor fluctuations in oxygenated (HbO) and deoxygenated (HbR) hemoglobin within the prefrontal cortex during learning activities.

Interactive Simulator

Test different study methods, view real-time cortical curves, complete quizzes, and calculate your Cognitive Efficiency Index.

Personalized Insights

Combine physiological measurements with quiz performance to generate personalized, evidence-based study recommendations.

Privacy & Safety

User safety and data privacy are our top priorities. Your personal data is only gathered to be analysed. All the data gathered is stored safely; we ensure the highest level of privacy for our users through encrypted communication, secure data storage, and informed consent. MindMap is designed to support learning, never to diagnose medical conditions or label individuals.
